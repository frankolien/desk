## Description — 1,480/4,000 characters

Desk is a native iPhone app for discovering and following perpetual-futures traders on Monad.

See who is performing on Perpl from activity read directly from the exchange contract—not self-reported profiles. Explore open positions, compare traders, follow wallets and define how you want to react.

COPY WITH CONTROL
• Follow top traders ranked by win rate, profit factor, drawdown and capital at risk.
• Use Shadow mode to simulate copies without moving money.
• Switch to Live mode with your own sizing, leverage and risk rules.
• Keep protective orders on the venue, even after Desk is closed.

SEE THE MARKET
• View long-versus-short positioning across supported markets.
• Track wallets and receive activity notifications.
• Review collateral, open positions and recent activity in one place.
• Share position results with customizable cards.

SECURED BY FACE ID
Desk uses a passkey to derive signing material when needed. There is no seed phrase and no separate wallet app. The trading key is held in memory while Desk is open and is not stored on the device.

Desk includes Home Screen widgets, Live Activities and push alerts so important activity is visible without keeping the app open.

IMPORTANT
This release operates on Monad testnet with test assets only. No real-money trading is available. Perpetual futures are leveraged and involve significant risk. Market data may be delayed or unavailable, and simulated or past performance does not guarantee future results.
