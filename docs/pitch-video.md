# Pitch video: script

The form asks for up to **two minutes**: the team, the problem, and why you are building it. This
is you on camera or your voice over the product, not a second demo. Fill the brackets before
recording; everything else is true of the build as it stands.

Record in one take if you can. Phone propped at eye level, daylight from the front, the app
running on a second phone or in the simulator for the two cutaways. About 270 words at a
speaking pace lands at 1:50.

## 0:00–0:15 · Who

**Shot:** you.

> "I'm [name], [one line about you: where you're from, what you've built or done]. I built Desk
> for Monad Metropolis, [alone / with name], in [number] weeks."

## 0:15–0:45 · The problem

**Shot:** you, then a cutaway to any wallet app's seed-phrase screen or a hardware wallet if you
have one to hold up.

> "Perps are the most traded thing in crypto, and trading them from a phone is still a chore.
> A seed phrase. A wallet app. An extension. Then a website in a shell. And the one thing people
> actually want, to follow the traders who are winning, is a bot you cannot look inside. You
> find out whether it works by losing money. Meanwhile the chain already knows who is winning.
> Every trade on Perpl is public. Nobody had turned that into something you could hold."

## 0:45–1:25 · What Desk is

**Shot:** cutaway to Home with live prices, then the leaderboard, then back to you.

> "Desk is a native iPhone app for perps on Monad. Face ID is the account: a Mera passkey
> derives the wallet that holds your AUSD and the key that signs your orders. The wallet key is
> never stored. The order key cannot withdraw and is sealed to your face on this phone. AUSD is
> the only money you ever see, and every trade runs on Perpl.
>
> Then the part I built it for. Desk reads every trader off Perpl's exchange contract, scores
> them on their record, and copies their moves the moment their trade lands on the chain, under
> your rules, in shadow first. Nobody submits a track record. The chain is the track record.
>
> And the same passkey opens the same desk in a browser."

## 1:25–1:50 · Why me, why now

**Shot:** you.

> "[One honest sentence about why you care: what you traded, what went wrong, or who you built
> this for.] Monad is fast enough that a copy can land in the same block as the trade it follows,
> and Agora's AUSD means the balance on screen is a dollar, not a token. Those two things made
> this the right moment.
>
> Everything in the demo is on mainnet with real money: deposit, trade, close, withdraw. It is on
> TestFlight today, with 542 Swift tests and 265 server tests behind it. That's Desk."

## Rules

- Say "mainnet" and "real money" once each, where they are true. Never imply returns; "the
  traders who are winning" describes a leaderboard.
- No slides. Two cutaways at most, both of the real app.
- End on your face or the app, not a logo.
