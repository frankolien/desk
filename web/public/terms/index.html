What Desk is

Desk is an iPhone app, a web app at trydesk.trade, a public API, and a plugin that lets assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude read Desk’s market data. By using any of them you agree to these terms.

Desk is software. It is not a bank, broker, exchange, custodian or investment adviser, and it never holds your funds. Trades happen on venues Desk connects to, such as Perpl on Monad, and settle on public blockchains.

You must be 18 or older to trade with Desk, and you are responsible for following the laws where you live.

Your keys

Your wallet comes from a passkey on your device. Desk never sees your keys and can’t recover them. If you lose your passkey and its backup, nobody can restore access to your funds, including us.

Every trade, transfer and copy rule you confirm is yours. Transactions on a blockchain can’t be reversed.

Trading risk

Perpetual futures are leveraged. A small move against you can liquidate a position and you can lose everything you put in. Copying another trader copies their losses too, and a copy can fill at a different price than theirs, or not at all.

Venues, bridges and networks can be slow, congested or down. Desk can’t promise an order is filled, a stop is triggered or a transfer arrives at a given time.

Information, not advice

Everything Desk shows, in the app, the API or an assistant, is market information: prices, positions, holdings, trading records, risk ratings and rankings. None of it is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything.

The data comes from blockchains and outside services and can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Risk ratings are checks against rules, not guarantees. A trader’s or a wallet’s past results don’t predict their next trade. Make your own decisions.

The API and the plugin

The API and the plugin are read-only and free to use within their rate limits. Please don’t try to get around those limits, overload the service, resell its data as your own service, or use it to harass anyone. We may limit or block traffic that does.

Answers an assistant gives using Desk’s data are written by that assistant, not by Desk. We may change, pause or retire endpoints and tools.
