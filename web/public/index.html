Signals

Read from the chain, not reported.

Every trader on the board is read off Perpl’s exchange contract — the account, its position bitmap, each open row and its mark. Nobody submits their record and nobody edits it.

  • 1

    Scored on what survives

    Win rate, profit factor and drawdown, weighted by money actually at risk.

  • 2

    Rules before money

    Shadow or live, follow or fade, fixed or conviction sizing, and a leverage cap — written before a cent moves.

  • 3

    Stops live on the venue

    Placed on Perpl, not in the app, so a copy stays protected long after Desk is closed.

Identity

Your face is the trading key.

One passkey derives both the wallet that holds the collateral and the key that signs every order, on demand. Face ID isn’t a lock screen over a saved secret — there is no saved secret.

  • 1

    No seed phrase

    Nothing to write down, photograph, or lose.

  • 2

    Nothing stored

    The key lives in memory while the app is open and is gone when it closes.

  • 3

    Gasless orders

    Signed on the device, sent over Perpl’s websocket, deadline-bound to the head block.

Security

No server can trade for anyone.

Including ours. The signing key exists only in the app’s memory, only while it is open and unlocked. Ours pushes notifications and reads public chain data — nothing it holds could move your money.
