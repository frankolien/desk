Identity

Your face is the trading key.

One passkey derives both the wallet that holds the collateral and the key that signs every order. Face ID is not a lock screen laid over a stored secret — there is no stored secret. The key is derived on demand, lives in memory while the app is open, and is gone when it closes.

  • No seed phrase to write down, photograph or lose
  • No wallet connect, no extension, no clipboard
  • Backed up by iCloud Keychain, like every other passkey
  • Onboarding says plainly what losing the phone means

Signals

A leaderboard that is read, not reported.

Every trader on it is read from Perpl's exchange contract on Monad — the account, its position bitmap, each open row and its mark. Their history is indexed from the chain's own position events, so nobody submits their record and nobody edits it.

  • Live open PnL, portfolio value and position count
  • Closed trades, win rate, profit factor, worst drawdown
  • Style tags read off behaviour: scalper, swing, degen leverage
  • A score weighted by money actually at risk, so dust scalpers do not top the board

Copy engine

Rules before money.

Copying is not a switch, it is a set of limits you write first. Shadow mode fills every copy at the live mainnet mark, charges real taker fees both ways against a 1,000 AUSD paper balance and liquidates where the venue would — so a strategy can be proved before a cent moves.

Accountability

It measures itself.

Every copy records the seconds between their move and your fill, and the slippage against their entry in basis points. Shadow and live are kept apart and never added together. When a figure cannot be read, the app says so instead of showing you a stale one — an unreadable position book is never treated as an empty one, because that would announce closes that never happened.

  • Realised result, win rate, copies and average time to fill
  • Every skip logged with the reason it was skipped
  • A share card for the result, generated on device

The desk

And a desk of your own.

Copying is one way to use Desk. The other is to trade it yourself: the markets Perpl lists, each with its own leverage ceiling, a ticket that tells you what is missing before you can send it, and a hold to confirm rather than a tap to regret.

  • Collateral, open position, entry and liquidation distance on one screen
  • Affordability checked before Face ID, not discovered at the venue
  • Stops and take profits rounded toward the mark on both sides
  • A stop placed behind your liquidation price is refused, not sent

Security

No server can trade for anyone.

Including ours. The signing key exists only in the app's memory, only while it is open and unlocked. That is the reason live copying needs Desk running, and it is a deliberate trade, not a shortcut.

What the server does

Pushes notifications, reads public chain data and indexes trader history. Nothing it holds could move your money.

What ships in the binary

No secrets. Keys and tokens live only in the server's environment, and every contract address is pinned per network rather than trusted from a response.

What happens on sign-out

Balances, positions, copy state and push subscriptions are cleared together, so the next face on the phone inherits nothing.

Rounding always goes against the trader, never in their favour, and the arithmetic lives in a module that cannot reach the network by construction.
